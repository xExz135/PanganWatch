## Evidence
Query:
    select date, count(*) as n_provinces from fct_daily_price
    where market_tier = 'traditional' and commodity_id = 'beras_medium_1'
    group by 1 having count(*) < 27 order by 1;
Result: 25 days, e.g. 2022-04-29 (2 provinces), 2022-05-04 (1), 2022-05-05 (2), 2022-05-06 (2),
2022-05-16 (1), 2023-12-25 (5). Several sit around Lebaran 2022, inside the A5 window; on
2022-04-29 the national rice "price" jumped from Rp 11,788 to Rp 13,750 and back.

## Impact
Published in the repo only (findings.md and the local dashboard; not posted, not deployed).
After dropping thin days (new `ana_national_price`: at least 80% of the usual provinces), the
A5 headline numbers moved by at most 3 points: bird's-eye chili in Ramadan week 1 +35% → +34%;
shallots in the last week before Lebaran +6% → +3%, 2–4 weeks after +16% → +17%. The "4 of 5
years" and "all five years" statements still hold. No other role reads these tables.
